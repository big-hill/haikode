Sprocket Control Panel
======================

Version: 1.2

The control panel exposes three sliders and one toggle. The toggle is
persisted between launches; the sliders are not.

Keyboard shortcuts
------------------

  Alt-Q   quit
  Alt-,   settings
  Alt-R   reset the sliders to their defaults

Known issues
------------

The reset shortcut does not clear the toggle. This is deliberate: the
toggle belongs to the document, not to the panel.
